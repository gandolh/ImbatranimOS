## Context

The cutover (commit `fb3de23`) deleted the auth module but left its
dependencies, types, helpers and prose behind. Each item below was checked for
live readers and has none.

**Dependencies**
- `argon2`, `otplib`, `qrcode` in `apps/backend/package.json` — zero imports.
  `argon2` is a native module, compiled into every prod image by the
  Dockerfile's `proddeps` stage (`npm ci --omit=dev --workspace=backend`), with
  prebuilds for seven platforms the image never runs on. The three together are
  about 2.8 MB of dead weight against the "lightweight is identity" invariant.
  Their satellites: `allowScripts.argon2` in the root `package.json`, and
  `otplib|@otplib|@scure|@noble` in the Jest `transformIgnorePatterns` of both
  `apps/backend/package.json` and `apps/backend/test/jest-e2e.json` (keep
  `jose`).
- `cookie-parser`: `main.ts` registers it, but nothing reads `req.cookies` —
  Ward reads the raw `Cookie` header. Remove the middleware and the dependency
  (`@types/cookie-parser` too).
- Unrelated drift found in the same pass: `zustand` in
  `apps/add-ons/calendar/package.json` and `dayjs` in
  `apps/add-ons/logs/package.json` are never imported.

**Dead code**
- `apps/backend/src/modules/auth/dto/auth.dto.ts` (setup, login and TOTP DTOs).
- `apps/core/src/modules/auth/lib/passwordChange.ts` and its 8-case test —
  only its own test imports it.
- `toSignIns` / `SignIn` (`packages/ui/src/lib/systemLog.ts:32-51`, re-exported
  from `packages/ui/src/index.ts:46-47` and
  `apps/core/src/shared/lib/systemLog.ts`) and the ten `auth.*` labels in
  `apps/add-ons/logs/src/lib/logFormat.ts`. No backend code emits `auth.*`
  events any more; update `logFormat.test.ts` to match.
- `apps/backend/src/modules/notes/notes.service.ts` — imported by no module,
  and written against the pre-brief-94 `recent_files` shape.

**Prose that describes deleted behaviour**
- `SessionAuthGuard` (the guard is `WardAuthGuard`) in the doc comments of
  `modules/auth/public.decorator.ts`, `clock.controller.ts`,
  `todos.controller.ts`, `calendar.controller.ts`, `calendar.service.ts`,
  `files.controller.ts`, `logs.controller.ts`, `git.controller.ts`,
  `archive.controller.ts`, `http-proxy.controller.ts`,
  `http-proxy.service.ts`, `prefs.controller.ts`, and
  `packages/ui/src/system.ts`. (`ward.guard.ts` mentions it as history — keep
