## Context

The cutover (commit `fb3de23`) deleted the auth module but left its
dependencies, types, helpers and prose behind. Each item below was checked for
live readers and has none.

**Dependencies**
- `argon2`, `otplib`, `qrcode` in `apps/backend/package.json` — zero imports.
  `argon2` is a native module, compiled into every prod image by the
  Dockerfile's `proddeps` stage (`npm ci --omit=dev --workspace=backend`), with
  prebuilds for seven platforms the image never runs on. The three together are
  about 2.8 MB of dead weight against the "lightweight is identity" invariant.
  Their satellites: `allowScripts.argon2` in the root `package.json`, and
  `otplib|@otplib|@scure|@noble` in the Jest `transformIgnorePatterns` of both
  `apps/backend/package.json` and `apps/backend/test/jest-e2e.json` (keep
  `jose`).
- `cookie-parser`: `main.ts` registers it, but nothing reads `req.cookies` —
  Ward reads the raw `Cookie` header. Remove the middleware and the dependency
  (`@types/cookie-parser` too).
- Unrelated drift found in the same pass: `zustand` in
  `apps/add-ons/calendar/package.json` and `dayjs` in
  `apps/add-ons/logs/package.json` are never imported.
